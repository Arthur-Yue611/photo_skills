# Photo to Color Fog prompts

Use the actual original. Fill the warm-light direction and source-specific constraints before generation.

## 中文

请编辑我上传的原始照片，每张单独输出一张摄影作品。保留原始宽高比例、取景、主体身份、人物数量、脸部五官与眼镜、发型、姿态、身形、服装结构、构图、透视、环境布局与材质，只改变空气、光线和曝光氛围。

将完整景深置于高密度浓雾中，前景也有可见空气介质，中远景进一步被雾吞没。根据原图光线和空间选择冷暖方向：一侧为明确的深青绿、蓝绿色冷雾，色相清晰且保持冷感；另一侧由隐藏的画外强暖逆光穿透浓雾，使空气形成明亮浓郁的橙金、琥珀色发光半圆形雾晕，最亮处金黄，向暗部过渡为橙褐。雾晕是空气中的宽柔发光体积，不画出太阳圆盘、圆形轮廓或人物头顶光环。

冷暖两侧保持纯净、饱满的色彩密度，不被灰雾中和，仅在主体附近柔和渗透，不形成硬分割，也不把整幅画面平均混成灰色。主体保持低调曝光，正面偏暗，仅发丝和局部轮廓被暖光强穿透；黑位干净，暗部保留适量细节，人物仍可辨认。浓雾降低背景反差和细节，但保留近处主体的材质。柔感来自空气散射、宽柔辉光和空气中的柔亮薄光，而非整图模糊、全局去饱和或灰色蒙层。依照原图质感适量提升对比度。

地面保持干燥哑光，不增加积水、湿地或暖色倒影。原片若有湖面，应保留湖面原位置，允许浓雾遮远景，不把湖面改成陆地，也不额外生成强烈金色倒影带。原片已经湿润而与此规则冲突时，先说明，不擅自改变材质。

禁止脏灰、粗糙颗粒、米灰雾、色彩发白、色彩平均化、硬光束、HDR、过度锐化、新增人物或无关物体、改变脸型与姿态、文字、水印、边框、拼图和对比版式。

## English

Edit each original into one separate photographic image. Preserve the original aspect ratio, framing, person count and identity, face shape and features, glasses, hairstyle, pose, body proportions, clothing structure, composition, perspective, environment layout and materials. Change only atmosphere, lighting and exposure.

Fill the full depth with dense fog, including visible air in the foreground. Let middle and distant scenery progressively disappear into fog. Choose the light direction from the source: one side is distinctly cool deep teal/blue-green; on the other, a hidden strong off-frame warm backlight creates a broad soft half-round luminous volume of amber and orange-gold air. Use golden yellow at its brightest area and orange-brown toward darker fog. Do not draw a sun disk, circular outline or head halo.

Keep both colors rich, clean and distinct. Let them blend softly near the subject without neutralizing the frame into gray or pale beige. Keep the subject low-key, with dark front surfaces and warm transmission through hair and selected silhouette edges. Preserve clean blacks, modest shadow detail and recognizable people. Fog reduces background contrast and detail while nearby materials remain recognizable. Obtain softness through air scattering, broad gentle bloom and veiling glare, not global blur, desaturation or a gray overlay. Adjust contrast modestly to suit the source.

Keep ground dry and matte, without added puddles, wet pavement or warm reflected pools. Preserve existing lakes in their original positions; fog may obscure them but cannot turn them into land. Do not invent strong gold reflection bands. If an already-wet source conflicts with the dry-ground rule, explain the conflict before changing its surface.

No dirty gray or beige fog, washed-out color, averaged gray palette, coarse grain, hard light rays, HDR, oversharpening, extra people or objects, altered faces or poses, captions, watermarks, borders, comparison panels or collage.

## Targeted corrections

- Fog too gray: keep geometry and subjects fixed; strengthen deep teal and amber in the air without bleaching blacks.
- Subject too bright: lower front illumination while retaining hair/edge backlight and recognizable facial structure.
- Glow looks like a sun: remove the disk/outline and spread soft luminous fog from outside the frame.
- New wet ground/reflections: restore original dry matte materials; retain genuine source water.
- Face drift: revise only the changed facial features using the original as identity authority; do not repaint the whole scene.
