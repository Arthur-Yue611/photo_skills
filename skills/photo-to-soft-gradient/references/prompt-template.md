# Lower-panel prompt

Use the actual photo as the content reference. Fill in the scene-specific fields; do not send the complete final-image prompt to a generator when the top must be untouched.

## Prompt

Create ONLY the lower artwork from the attached original photo, near a 144:175 portrait ratio. Do not create a photo strip, upper panel, comparison or multi-panel layout.

Keep these recognizable details: [main detail, supporting shape, original colors].
Keep subject count, species, pose and important contacts: [observations].
Rebuild the lower composition by [one main change of scale, position or extension]. Preserve the subject's own proportions and meaningful relationships; do not copy the original full layout.

Let 3–5 overlapping free-form color fields dominate the image. Vary their sizes and positions. Each slowly fades from its colored center into the surrounding color; blend edges completely and mix intersections naturally. Use [3–5 source-related colors], on a [deep blue / dark violet / deep green / actual dark source color] base. Keep the shadows colored, the broad highlights soft, and [area] quiet and open.

Let at least one field cross [part of the subject]. Allow that outer shape to dissolve into translucent color light. Keep [few selected details] clear enough to recognize the original. Give the image a clean, soft, light, slightly floating feeling. Do not apply blur to the whole image.

Protect visible faces: keep the original facial features, expression, glasses, face shape and skin color. Do not redraw, beautify, rotate, recolor or dissolve facial cores. Place the source face later with an opaque mask if exact preservation is required. Build color fields around it.

No extra people, new objects, duplicated heads, neon outlines, star fields, dense smoke, rough grains, paper fibers, torn edges, folds, frames, words, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI or unrelated decorations.

## Scene plan example

Source: an orange flower with a dark center on a dim green background.
Keep: the dark flower center, the petal junction and the real orange petal shape.
Rebuild: enlarge the single flower and move it below/right of center; extend one outer petal into translucent light.
Fields: large amber above/right, smaller teal below/left, muted violet behind the flower, and a quiet dark green base.
Keep clear: center and two petal edges. Dissolve: a peripheral petal edge crossed by the amber field.
Add no extra flower or stem; do not turn the petal into smoke.
