# Scene guide

Select the source detail and palette first. Make one clear change of scale or position, then build 3–5 color fields around it. Preserve a quiet area instead of filling every corner.

| Photo type | Keep recognizable | Lower-panel idea | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flowers | Center, petal junctions, species and main hue | Enlarge one real flower; soften outer petals into overlapping light | Added flowers, fake petals, a new center |
| Leaves and stems | Veins, leaf shape, petiole attachment | Extend an edge as translucent green light; float the existing leaf group | Detached stems, a different leaf species |
| Birds or animals | Species, pose, beak/ears, limbs and count | Lift the existing subject within a broad light field; retain a few detailed edges | Extra wings or legs, invented faces, new animals |
| Fish and underwater scenes | Real fish shapes and their number | Reorganize the group within blue-green fields without changing swimming poses | Added fish, fantasy sea creatures |
| Water and reflections | Shore, reeds, key reflection or ripple | Expand reflected color into broad fields; keep a thin recognizable shoreline | Stars, fake reflection directions, extra boats |
| Trees and woods | Main branch curve, trunks and leaf shape | Enlarge a real branch or canopy; let one edge fade into green/gold fields | Invented paths or buildings, added smoke |
| Night streets and lights | One street direction, building edge or lamp | Turn existing light into soft blue, violet or warm fields | Neon outlines, made-up signs, new traffic |
| Buildings, doors and windows | Distinct edge, opening, silhouette or shadow | Float a selected structural shape within source-colored light | Deformed geometry, false readable signs |
| Environmental portraits | Original face, hair, clothes, pose and contact | Change whole-subject scale/position; dissolve garment edges or surroundings | A new face, skin recoloring, extra people |
| Dense groups, ID photos, exact maps | Too many essential details for this style | Explain the fit problem or choose a different requested treatment | Claiming faithful results while discarding essential details |

## Color rules

Choose dark and bright colors from the photograph's light and palette. Strengthen them gently if needed for the lower art; never adjust the top. A nearly monochrome source can use related shades and subdued neighboring hues, without forcing a rainbow.

For black-and-white sources, use a restrained chosen base color and a few related low-saturation fields below. Identify that artistic color choice; the untouched top remains black-and-white.

Vary field size and spacing. Prefer a large quiet field, a medium field and one to three smaller ones. The field count describes the composition, not a precise number of colors after mixing.

## Shape rules

Keep a single petal or leaf connected to its original support unless the requested surreal change clearly involves floating. Keep meaningful human or animal contacts intact even if the surrounding scene is rearranged.

Scale subjects uniformly. Do not stretch a person or animal to fit. Keep a retained face at its original orientation and expression; move its whole person consistently. If a face cannot remain faithful after a proposed change, choose another layout.

## References versus instructions

Style screenshots may contain bright pastel examples, water reflections or silk-like flows. Use them to understand soft transitions and restrained detail. Follow the user's requested dark colored base, 3–5 broad fields and no unrelated effects. Do not copy a creator's labels, account name, interface, watermark or exact existing artwork.
