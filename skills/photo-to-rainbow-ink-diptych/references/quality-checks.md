# Acceptance checks

## Photograph and layout

- Use an actual original, not a style screenshot or previous generated artwork.
- Verify 9:16 final and 16:9 top. Defaults: 1152×2048, 1152×648, 1152×1400.
- Inspect the top crop for important faces, gestures and relationships; cropping is required, so do not claim the whole original remains visible.
- Confirm deterministic EXIF orientation/crop/uniform resize, with no generative processing, tone adjustment, enhancement or retouching above.
- Verify saved top pixels equal the prepared crop. Equality refers to the resized/cropped source, not the unscaled original file. Preserve original bytes separately.
- Keep all tears, folds, blending and shadows below the seam; do not add photo margins or cover the top rectangle.

## Lower art

- Recognize the same scene and main subjects, with unchanged counts, positions, pose, facing direction and depth relationships.
- Check source light direction, color travel and fold shadows.
- Check large wet blooms, ink anchors, mineral granulation, rough fibers and quiet exposed paper; avoid a smooth generic filter.
- Check seven hues in full-spectrum mode with source colors dominant; avoid seven equal bands or saturation everywhere.
- Keep folds few, coherent, motivated and subordinate.
- Retain people's contours and clothes without invented facial detail. Inspect protected composites separately if photographic faces are requested below too.
- Remove text, numbers, watermarks, logos, interface icons, frames, swatches, captions, stars, synthetic smoke, invented fog and unrelated ornaments.
- Check art cropping preserves subjects and pigment direction; never stretch to fit.

## Revision

Fix the lower art only, reassemble with the same original/crop, and verify top pixels again. Never apply generative edits to the completed diptych.
